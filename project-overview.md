# Project Overview — EV Charging Site Dashboard

## Background

Walmart is expanding its nationwide EV charging infrastructure, requiring dozens of coordinated steps per store: legal title review, site planning, utility applications, and real estate approvals. Managing this across hundreds of locations in a shared Excel spreadsheet created visibility gaps and reporting delays.

This Power BI solution was built to provide a single source of truth — a live, filterable, drillable dashboard consumed by operations managers, real estate teams, and executive stakeholders.

---

## Problem Statement

| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Status tracked manually in Excel | No real-time visibility |
| No easy way to segment stores by priority | High-priority sites not surfaced quickly |
| Stage bottlenecks invisible | Delays compounded without early detection |
| No geographic view | Regional patterns missed |
| Average duration per stage unknown | No baseline for timeline planning |

---

## Solution

A multi-page Power BI report with a **live connection** to the Teams Excel sheet, delivering:

- Priority-ranked store tables segmented into three tiers
- Full pipeline tracking for Title, Memo, Site Plan, and Utility stages
- Store-level drill-through from any chart
- Google Maps hyperlinks per store
- Interactive map with geospatial plotting
- Pie chart summaries for quick at-a-glance completion rates

---

## Stakeholders

| Role | How They Use the Report |
|------|------------------------|
| EV Program Manager | Monitor overall site progress, identify delays |
| Real Estate Team | Track title and site plan stages per region |
| Store Operations | Look up individual store status and contacts |
| Executive Leadership | Pie chart summaries and map view for rollout health |

---

## Outcome

- Eliminated manual status consolidation across teams
- Reduced time-to-identify bottlenecks from days to seconds
- Provided geo-visual context for regional planning decisions
- Enabled store managers to be contacted directly from the report

---
